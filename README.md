Movie Data Analysis (2012-2016)
Genre Dominance:

Insight: "Action" movies are the primary revenue engine, generating over $28 Billion—nearly double that of the next closest genre (Comedy).

Decision: Continue prioritizing high-budget "Action" projects and franchises, as they provide the highest total market share and revenue volume.

Profitability Efficiency:

Insight: While Action earns the most in total, "Adventure" and "Family" genres yield the highest average profit per movie.

Decision: Diversify the portfolio by investing in high-quality "Adventure" and "Family" films. These genres offer a better Return on Investment (ROI) with potentially lower risks than massive action blockbusters.

Market Trends (Yearly Performance):

Insight: 2014 was a peak year for the industry with $20 Billion in revenue, followed by a gradual decline through 2016.

Decision: Analyze the 2014 release slate (specifically successful sequels or original IPs) to replicate the marketing and scheduling strategies that drove that year's success.
